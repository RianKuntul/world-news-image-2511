Entry for the make.fun **Sum up 25 November's world news in one image** competition — one editorial image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026. The program copies the committed image to `/output/entry.png`, byte-for-byte, with a size guard, a whole-image format check, and a sha256 self-check.

## The day's news

The image covers the five stories that defined 25 November 2026:

- **Pakistan's first astronaut launches to Tiangong** — Muhammad Zeeshan Ali lifts off aboard China's Shenzhou-24, the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut aboard the Tiangong space station.
- **Huawei found guilty in a U.S. trial** — a New York jury convicts the Chinese tech giant on 10 of 12 counts, including trade-secrets theft and bank fraud.
- **CIA and the U.S. spy chief hit by a cyberattack** — the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence are hit by an intrusion affecting classified information.
- **Putin warns retaliation is "inevitable"** after Ukraine keeps striking Russian oil refineries.
- **About 175 migrants missing** after a boat from Senegal vanishes en route to Europe.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to choose where `entry.png` is written (defaults to `/output`).

## The entry

`entry.png` — a 1024×1024 editorial collage: the Shenzhou-24 launch at dawn, the Huawei courtroom, a cyber operations room under attack, Putin before a burning refinery, and a migrant boat at sea, under the masthead "WEDNESDAY 25 NOVEMBER 2026".
